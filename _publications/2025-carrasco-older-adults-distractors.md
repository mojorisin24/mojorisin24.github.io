This study examines how aging affects vulnerability to distractors during working memory encoding. Older adults exhibit significantly reduced WM accuracy and increased distractor interference compared to younger adults, highlighting age-related declines in attentional control.

**Published:** *Psychological Research* (2026). [https://doi.org/10.1007/s00426-025-02197-y](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00426-025-02197-y)
