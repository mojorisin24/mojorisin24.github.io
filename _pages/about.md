I am a Postdoctoral Scholar in the Brain Imaging and EEG Lab at the University of California, San Francisco and the San Francisco VA Health Care System, where I work with Dr. Daniel Mathalon, PhD MD. My research uses EEG/ERP recordings, multivariate pattern analysis (MVPA), and principal component analysis (PCA) to study neural habituation and cognitive processing in individuals at clinical high risk for psychosis, with the broader goal of improving biomarker fidelity and developing reliable, scalable tools for earlier detection and intervention in schizophrenia.

I completed my PhD in the Neuroscience Graduate Group at UC Davis in 2024, where I trained with Dr. Steven J. Luck at the Center for Mind and Brain. My doctoral work combined experimental and computational approaches — including machine learning applied to electrophysiological data — to investigate the neural mechanisms underlying working memory, selective attention, learning, and decision-making. I published work spanning both basic cognitive neuroscience and EEG methodology.

Outside of the lab, I enjoy exploring the outdoors, playing guitar, and spending time with friends.
