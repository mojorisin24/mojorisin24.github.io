Presented findings on distinct visual and auditory habituation profiles in clinical high-risk (CHR) youth, revealing modality-specific neural adaptation differences. Using auditory and visual P300 ERPs from the Accelerating Medicines Partnership® Schizophrenia (AMP® SCZ) dataset, we identified neural habituation deficits that may serve as biomarkers for psychosis risk.
